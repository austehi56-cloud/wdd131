Budgeting Basics

A budget is simply a plan for your money before you spend it. Tracking your income and expenses prevents overspending and ensures your bills are always covered.

How to Track Income and Expenses

  • Calculate Net Income: Focus on your take-home pay after taxes and deductions.
  • Log Fixed Expenses: List recurring monthly bills like rent, utilities, internet, and transport.
  • Track Variable Spending: Monitor daily purchases such as groceries, dining out, and entertainment for 30 days.

The 40/40/20 Rule Example

One of the easiest ways to divide your monthly take-home pay is the 40/40/20 framework. Here is how a $2,000 monthly paycheck breaks down:

Category Percentage Monthly Amount Examples
Needs 40% $800 Rent, groceries, utilities, basic transport, minimum loan payments
Wants 40% $800 Eating out, streaming subscriptions, hobbies, shopping
Savings & Debt 20% $400 Emergency fund, high-yield savings, extra debt repayment
