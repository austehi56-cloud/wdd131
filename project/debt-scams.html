Avoiding Debt Traps & Financial Scams

Protecting your money is just as important as earning it. Knowing how to spot predatory lenders and digital fraud keeps your finances safe.

Red Flags of Risky Loans

  • Sky-High APRs: Payday loans and instant loan apps often charge triple-digit annual interest rates that trap borrowers in cycles of debt.
  • Hidden Fees: Watch out for origination fees, late penalties, and mandatory insurance add-ons buried in fine print.
  • "No Credit Check, Guaranteed Approval": Legitimate lenders always verify your ability to repay.

How to Spot Online Fraud

Golden Rule: Never share your One-Time Passwords (OTP), PINs, or full card details with anyone claiming to be from your bank or customer support.
  • Urgent Phishing Messages: Scammers create false panic ("Your account will be suspended in 2 hours") to make you click fake links.
  • Too-Good-To-Be-True Investments: Promises of guaranteed daily or weekly double-digit returns are classic Ponzi or crypto scam tactics.
  • Advance-Fee Job or Loan Scams: Never pay an upfront "processing fee" to receive a loan or secure a job offer.

Rules for Responsible Borrowing

Only borrow for assets that grow your income or net worth (such as education or essential work tools), compare total repayment costs across multiple regulated banks, and keep total monthly debt payments under 15–20% of your net income.
