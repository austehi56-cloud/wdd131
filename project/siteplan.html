Maple Street Community Garden

Site Name

Maple Street Community Garden — the name of the neighborhood garden the site represents, so visitors recognize it right away. The sprout logo above will appear in the header of every page.

Possible domain: maplestreetgarden.org

Site Purpose

The site is an information hub for garden members and neighbors. It provides a seasonal planting guide, a list of available garden plots, and a form to request a plot or sign up to volunteer.

Scenarios

  • What vegetables can I plant this month?
  • Are any garden plots available, and how much do they cost?
  • How do I sign up to volunteer on a work day?
